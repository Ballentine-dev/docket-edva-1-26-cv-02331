# Ballentine v. Hall, No. 1:26-cv-02331 (E.D. Va.)

The docket as JSON, so work can read the text here instead of pulling CourtListener or viewing PDF images.

`master-docket.json` has three parts:

- `case`: court, judges, parties and case details.
- `docket_through_ecf_108`: the Master Docket built by Muse from CourtListener on November 1, 2026. ECF Nos. 1 through 108, with document text where it was freely available. The `counts` and `notes` fields say what was and was not available. CourtListener's copy was last updated October 30, 2026.
- `packages_november_1_2026`: the ten filing packages, word for word, from `261101_All_Packages.json`. These are the papers as prepared, not docketed copies, and they have no ECF numbers yet. The status field in this part dates from before filing. The notices of hearing are not included; they have not been filed.

Apart from the notices of hearing, both source files are unchanged inside this file.
